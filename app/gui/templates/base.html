How to read this screen — the marks and the six levels

Where a number came from

52.71 GB — measured. Something walked the folder or read the bucket, on a date it will tell you.

~11.6 GB — assumed. Dotted, and printed dimmer, because it rests on a guess you made.

$3.98 — invoiced. This is the bill Amazon actually sent.

$1.85 — projected, no line at all. A projection takes the weakest mark of everything that fed it.

How loudly it is telling you

Note — background explanation. No box, no colour, nothing to do.
AdviceTake it or leave it. Usually comes with a one-click accept.
WarningWill cost you money or surprise you later. Nothing is stopped.
BlockerStops the button. Names the two ways out and jumps you to the control.
DoneOnly ever a moment after you press something. Then it clears itself.
FailedA run that ended badly. Carries a time, the exact error, and a record you can open. Never shares a shape with Done.
