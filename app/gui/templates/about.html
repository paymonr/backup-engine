The tools this app drives

restic
restic — makes the snapshot backups and keeps their history in one encrypted store.
rclone
rclone — makes the plain copies, file for file.
the file-history catalog
the file-history catalog — this app's own index of every version of every file, one small database per job.
OpenTofu
OpenTofu — creates the bucket and the key, only if you chose the automated setup.
supercronic
supercronic — runs the schedule inside the container.
